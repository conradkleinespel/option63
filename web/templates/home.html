Share contacts on your terms.

option63 rewrites vCard data at the point where it leaves your control — before it reaches personal devices, untrusted clouds, or AI ingestion. Only what you allow ever crosses the line.

How it works

option63 rewrites each vCard at the boundary — sensitive fields are removed, masked, or relayed before the contact ever reaches an untrusted device, cloud, or AI agent.
